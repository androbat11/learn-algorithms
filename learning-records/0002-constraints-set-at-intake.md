# Constraints fixed at intake

TypeScript is the interview language. Timeline is roughly three months, targeting
December 2026. Cadence is daily at about twenty minutes.

The daily cadence is the most consequential of these: it is close to ideal for
spaced retrieval, so lessons should be built as a chain — each one opening with a
short retrieval check on the previous one — rather than as standalone units. It
also hard-caps lesson length. Twenty minutes means roughly 900–1400 words plus
one drill, and a lesson that overruns will simply not get done.

The three-month horizon rules out building foundations bottom-up. Teach the
process first, then patterns in NeetCode roadmap order, and accept gaps in the
theory.
